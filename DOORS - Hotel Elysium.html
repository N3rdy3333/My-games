THE HOTEL

Every locked door needs a key hidden somewhere in its room. Check the drawers, the tables, the beds.
When the lights flicker and buzz, something is coming. Hide in a closet and hold your breath, but stay too long and you suffocate.
If you see a glowing blue door, something waits beyond it. Look down and never meet its eyes.

TOGETHER

Unlocked and opened doors are shared with everyone in your lobby. Each of you faces your own horrors, so watch your friends' flashlights through the dark.
