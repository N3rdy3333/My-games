BLOCKCRAFT

A tiny voxel world. You'll spawn somewhere grassy or sandy along the coast. Punch trees for wood, dig into the hillside for stone, and craft planks, sticks, tools and blocks from what you gather. Every world is built from a seed: the same seed always gives the same 1280x1280-block map.
